## 🌷 About Flowy

**Flowy** is a warm, private, and completely free menstrual cycle companion that lives inside Telegram. It tracks your cycle, tells you which phase you're in, gently reminds you before your period arrives, and sends soft, encouraging messages along the way — no ads, no judgment, no data selling.

Think of Flowy as the caring friend who remembers your dates so you don't have to.

> 💬 **Try it now:** [**@flowycarebot**](https://t.me/flowycarebot)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🩸 **Cycle Tracking** — logs your period start date, cycle length, and period length
- ⏰ **Gentle Reminders** — nudges you 3 days, 1 day, and on the day your period is expected
- 🌙 **Phase Awareness** — knows whether you're Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory, or Luteal
- 💌 **Motivational Messages** — warm, phase-specific check-ins sent with `/motivation`
- 📊 **Live Status** — `/status` shows your current phase, day count, and next expected date
- 🔒 **Privacy by Design** — your data is yours; erase it anytime with `/deletedata`
- 🆓 **Completely Free** — no subscriptions, no premium tier, no ads

## 🤖 Bot Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/start` | Set up (or reset) your cycle profile |
| `/status` | See your current phase and next expected period |
| `/logperiod` | Log a new period start date |
| `/motivation` | Get a warm, phase-based message right now |
| `/deletedata` | Permanently erase all your stored data |
| `/help` | Show the full command list |

## 🔐 Privacy & Data

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Your%20Data-Stays%20Private-blueviolet?style=flat-square" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Delete-Anytime-success?style=flat-square" />

Flowy only stores what it needs to help you: your last period date, cycle length, and period length. Nothing is shared, sold, or used for anything beyond helping you understand your own body. One command — `/deletedata` — wipes everything, permanently.
